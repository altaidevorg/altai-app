## Gate evidence

The gate reads: *crash isolation, health, jobs, webhooks, scoped
secrets and idempotency.*

- **Crash isolation** — a `WorkerSupervisor` pure state machine over
  observations, driving real child processes (#815, #816): a worker
  that crashes or hangs is killed, observed, and replaced inside a
  restart budget, and an exhausted budget leaves the plugin down —
  never the host. Every behavior is exercised against re-exec'd real
  child processes, not mocks, on every CI platform.
- **Health** — `HealthProbePolicy` over the stdio transport (#817): an
  answered probe is the `Healthy` observation; silence inside the
  window kills the worker and is recorded as the crash it is; a
  replacement that answers is `Healthy`-from-`Crashed` from real
  evidence.
- **Jobs, idempotency** — at-most-once dispatch through the
  `DispatchLedger`, record-before-send (#818): a job id is sent once,
  ever; a real child's request counter proves a re-dispatch sends
  nothing (the worker saw 2 requests, not 3); ambiguous delivery stays
  visibly `Dispatched`, never silently retried.
- **Webhooks, scoped secrets** — the same at-most-once contract for
  deliveries in their own ledger family; secrets deliberately
  per-process — the host remembers every brokered value and re-hands
  the unhanded ones after a restart, so restarts are transparent to the
  plugin; `SecretString` cannot print itself (#820). Both surfaces and
  the jobs surface refuse a manifest without their declared capability
  before any state exists.
