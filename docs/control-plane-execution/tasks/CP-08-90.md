# CP-08-90 — qm provenance and evidence/replay conformance discovery

- **Package:** 084 — qm codebase (PR 1)
- **Status:** in progress
- **Depends on:** 045 (Evidence and governed delivery), 060 (Activity
  projections), and CP-08-89 (the preceding ordered adoption-track decision)

## Outcome

Locate and pin the authoritative qm source, revision, license and history.
Inventory only quality/evaluation mechanisms relevant to ALTAI evidence,
evaluation, comparison and replay. Map every candidate to ALTAI's canonical
Activity/Evidence ownership and publish an explicit adopt/defer/reject decision
before source is copied, enabled, or used to judge delivery.

## Acceptance

- Provenance and license are reproducible at a fixed source revision.
- Every quality/evaluation concept maps to an existing ALTAI contract or states
  its missing contract and risk.
- A deterministic benchmark defines evidence inputs, replay observations and
  pass/fail conditions before any runtime integration.
- No external evaluator becomes a system of record or delivery authority.
